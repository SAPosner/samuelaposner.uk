Amateur Radio!

My first dabble with Amateur Radio was taking part in a contact organised by my soon-to-be secondary school with Tim Peake whilst he was on the International Space Station. The contact - the first one in a series of 10 during his mission - was widely publicised at the time and it was very enjoyable to be part of the hubbub, even if in the end we suffered a fair bit of interference. I went on to earn my foundation licence - M6SQY
