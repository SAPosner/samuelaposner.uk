Amateur Radio!

My first dabble with Amateur Radio was taking part in a contact organised by my soon-to-be secondary school with Tim Peake whilst he was on the International Space Station. The contact - the first one in a series of 10 during his mission - was widely publicised at the time and it was very enjoyable to be part of the hubbub, even if in the end we suffered a fair bit of interference. I went on to earn my foundation licence - M6SQY - a couple of years later, before then earning my Intermediate licence - 2x0SPO. Whilst I am not as active now as I have been in the past, sticking mostly to VHF, I still really enjoy the hobby and - with more time - would like to get back to lower frequencies.

Using my secondary school's callsign (M0SCY) I've been able to take part in a number of RSGB contests. These are always great fun and a great challenge, espcially so when there's a group of you in the same room and you're all taking turns to help one another out. We never did fantastically but always had a good time which is far more important! I've also operated as part of the Youngsters on the Air programme. Again, having a special event callsign is always great for attracting some interesting contacts and I also enjoyed talking to student unfamiliar with amateur radio of the fun you can have through this hobby.
